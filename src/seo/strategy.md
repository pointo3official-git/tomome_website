# Tomome SEO Strategy

## 1. One-Sentence Description

Tomome is an online pregnancy support platform that helps expecting mothers feel more prepared and supported throughout pregnancy by providing pregnancy education, prenatal wellness, nutrition guidance, expert guidance, childbirth preparation, and ongoing support between routine antenatal visits.

Tomome complements routine antenatal care; it does not replace a woman's gynecologist, hospital, or other primary medical care.

---

## 2. Primary Audience

### Primary Audience

First-time pregnant women in Kerala, especially women aged approximately 24–35 living in urban and semi-urban areas who want more guidance, preparation, wellness support, and confidence during pregnancy beyond routine hospital visits.

### Secondary Audience

Pregnant women in Kerala looking for:

* Pregnancy education
* Prenatal yoga
* Nutrition guidance
* Childbirth preparation
* Psychological support
* Gynecological guidance
* Answers to pregnancy-related questions
* Practical support between antenatal appointments

### Audience Characteristics

They may be:

* First-time mothers
* Looking for pregnancy guidance online
* Searching for prenatal yoga and pregnancy exercise
* Looking for childbirth preparation
* Looking for pregnancy nutrition guidance
* Looking for emotional or psychological support
* Looking for practical day-to-day pregnancy guidance
* Looking for support in Malayalam and/or English

## 5. Core Problem Tomome Solves

Pregnant women already have routine antenatal visits, but they still need ongoing education, preparation, wellness support, expert guidance, and practical day-to-day support between visits.

### The Gap

A routine hospital appointment addresses important medical needs, but pregnancy also creates many questions between appointments:

* What should I eat?
* Is this exercise safe?
* How can I prepare for labour?
* What should I expect during childbirth?
* How can I prepare for the hospital?
* How can I prepare for breastfeeding?
* What should I know before the baby arrives?
* Where can I get trustworthy pregnancy education?
* Who can I ask when I have a question between appointments?

Tomome is designed to provide structured support around these needs while keeping the woman's gynecologist and hospital care at the center of medical decision-making.
